Scene 03, Conflict Register: a black-and-white Ikeda-style visualization of real Capitol Trades committee-risk data. One member of Congress is spotlit at a time. Their committees are listed at left, each with a mini jurisdiction barcode of the market sectors it oversees. A central spine shows an oversight strip on top and a disclosed-trade strip below across eleven sectors. A moving spotlight steps through each sector where the member both oversees and trades, drawing a bright flickering chain from the responsible committee, up to that sector, and down to the trade, ending on the single worst flagged trade with its risk score and flags. Flashing and glitch effects punctuate each lock. The score is a triage heuristic, not an allegation of unlawful conduct.
