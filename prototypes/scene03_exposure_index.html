Scene 03, Exposure Index: a black-and-white data instrument in the spirit of Ryoji Ikeda, driven by real Capitol Trades committee-risk data. A continuous mathematical carrier wave, binary margin streams and an instrument frame bind five movements that run as one machine. Movement one, the conflict census: every tracked member of Congress is a row of eleven market-sector cells rendered as a precise barcode field, scaled by disclosed dollar value, while a scanline hunts and locks one member. Movement two isolates and magnifies the locked row. Movement three, oversight by holdings: eleven dense sector columns, each carrying the member committee-oversight mask above and disclosed trades below, while a stepping needle sweeps the spectrum and strikes a crosshair on every sector where oversight and trading overlap, accumulating the conflicted dollar exposure. Movement four isolates the single worst flagged trade: giant ticker typography, the execution-to-disclosure reporting gap drawn as a calibrated timeline, risk flags stamped one by one, and a risk score counting to its value. Movement five purges the image into controlled block corruption and reassembles the census for the next member. The score is a triage heuristic, not an allegation of unlawful conduct.
