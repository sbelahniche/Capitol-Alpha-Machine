Scene 03, Exposure Index: a black-and-white data-art machine in the spirit of Ryoji Ikeda, driven by real Capitol Trades committee-risk data. The piece cycles continuously through five movements. First a census wall: every tracked member of Congress rendered as a flickering row of eleven market-sector cells while a scanline hunts and locks onto one member. The locked row is then extracted and blown up to full screen with glitch displacement. A sector spectrum follows: eleven dense barcode columns, one per market sector, with the member's committee-oversight mask above and disclosed trades below; a stepping needle sweeps the spectrum and strikes a crosshair on every sector where oversight and trading overlap, accumulating the conflicted dollar exposure. The verdict movement isolates the single worst flagged trade: giant ticker typography, the execution-to-disclosure reporting gap drawn as a calibrated timeline, risk flags stamped one by one, and a risk score counting to its value before a one-frame inversion flash. Finally the image corrupts into block noise and the census wall reassembles for the next member. The score is a triage heuristic, not an allegation of unlawful conduct.
