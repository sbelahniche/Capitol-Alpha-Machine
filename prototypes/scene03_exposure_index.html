Scene 03, Exposure Index: a monochrome data-light instrument combining a strict black-and-white data substrate with a subtle organic line mesh moving slowly from left to right, driven by real Capitol Trades committee-risk data. Layered contour lines and fine connective threads form a continuously deforming wave field behind a mathematical carrier wave, binary margin streams and an instrument frame. Five movements run as one machine. Conflict signals are drawn as bright white laser beams with additive glow. Movement one, the conflict census: every tracked member of Congress is a row of eleven market-sector cells rendered as a precise barcode field, scaled by disclosed dollar value, while a scanline hunts and locks one member. Movement two isolates and magnifies the locked row. Movement three, oversight by holdings: eleven dense sector columns, each carrying the member committee-oversight mask above and disclosed trades below, while a stepping needle sweeps the spectrum and strikes a crosshair on every sector where oversight and trading overlap, accumulating the conflicted dollar exposure. Movement four isolates the single worst flagged trade: giant ticker typography, the execution-to-disclosure reporting gap drawn as a calibrated timeline, risk flags stamped one by one, and a risk score counting to its value. Movement five purges the image into controlled block corruption and reassembles the census for the next member. The score is a triage heuristic, not an allegation of unlawful conduct.
